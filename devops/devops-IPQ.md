# DevOps Interview Preparation Questions (IPQ)

### Q: What is DevOps? Why is it important?
DevOps is a set of best practices, philosophies, and tools around the software development lifecycle (SDLC) aimed at continually improving processes and delivering value more efficiently. DevOps bridges the gap between development and operations teams, promoting collaboration, automation, and continuous feedback.

### Q: Why are feedback loops important in DevOps?
DevOps practices are fundamentally based on getting fast, continuous feedback and constantly improving processes. To achieve this, organizations must find and track the right metrics to measure their performance, identify bottlenecks, and validate the impact of improvements. Finding the ideal metrics for an organization is an iterative and ongoing journey.

### Q: What are some typical examples of DevOps metrics?
Some common DevOps metrics used to measure efficiency, speed, and stability include:
- **Deployment Frequency**: How often code is successfully deployed to production.
- **Lead Time for Changes**: The time it takes for a commit to reach production.
- **Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)**: The average time it takes to detect an incident.
- **Mean Time to Restore (MTTR)**: The average time it takes to recover from a product or service failure.
- **Change Failure Rate**: The percentage of deployments causing a failure in production.
- **Service Availability**: The uptime and reliability of critical services.
- **Code Coverage**: The percentage of code executed during automated testing.
- **Cost per Unit**: Operational cost efficiency metrics.
